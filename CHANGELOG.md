# Changelog

## 1.4.0

First public release of Skyfront.

- Drones: Mavic 3 Pro, Mavic 3T (thermal), P1-SUN interceptor (also thermal), FPV drone, Molniya strike drone.
- FPV drone carries a PG-7VM, Yasin or mortar shell; brick stand for take-off; GPS Betaflight OSD; analog video interference.
- Rate (acro) flight mode for the P1-SUN, FPV and Molniya; RC transmitter and gamepad support.
- Shahed-136 launched from the launch rail with route planning on the terminal map.
- Radar and air-raid siren.
- Client-side debris and explosion effects; long-range drone links up to 1000 blocks.
- Config moved to `config/skyfront-common.toml` with a section per drone; `/skyfront config reload`.
